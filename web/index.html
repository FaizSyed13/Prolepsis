What this is

A computer forecast for 20 large US companies, looking about one week ahead (the next 5 trading days). It learns from past prices and then adjusts for recent news headlines.

Chance of going up

Up 58% means that in similar past situations, the price ended the week higher about 58 times out of 100. Down is the rest. Most stocks sit between 50% and 60% because these companies rise in most weeks. Model up is the chance before the news was taken into account.

Price range

The 80% range is where the price should end up about 4 weeks out of 5. About 1 week in 5 it lands outside, sometimes far outside after big news. A wider range means a bumpier week is expected.

News

Headlines from the last 3 days are rated from −1 (all bad) to +1 (all good). Clearly good or bad news nudges the chance of going up by 3 points per step (lean up / lean down). Earnings news or a flood of strong headlines widens the range (Range x1.4 and similar). These news rules haven't been proven to help yet.

Buy, Hold or Sell

Click any stock to see two verdicts. Short term (next week): Buy if the chance of going up is at least 3 points above a normal week, Sell if at least 3 points below, otherwise Hold. Long term (a year or more): four simple checks (the price trend, the past year's gain or loss, analysts' price targets, and how expensive the stock is compared with its earnings), each counting +1 or −1. A total of +2 or more is Buy and −2 or less is Sell. A stock that's falling steeply never gets more than Hold.

How good is it, honestly?

In testing, the up/down calls were right about as often as simply guessing "up" every week, so treat them as a weak hint. The price ranges have been the most reliable part. Every forecast is saved and graded in How our past forecasts did, with nothing left out.

This is a research project, not financial advice. Nobody can reliably predict next week's stock prices. Never invest money you can't afford to lose.

Getting everything ready.

The first start downloads prices and the news-reading model, which can take a few minutes. After that it opens in about a minute.
