    How to read this page
    • Up / Down is the chance the price is higher or lower after 5 trading days.
    • Model up is the chance from the price model alone, before the news.
    • 80% range is where the price should end about 4 times out of 5. Big news can push it outside.
    • News (3 days) is FinBERT's overall rating of the last 3 days of headlines, from −1 (all bad) to +1 (all good), with the number of headlines.
    • Lean up / lean down moves the chance of going up by 3 points per step when the news is clearly good or bad.
    • Range x1.4 and similar widen the range when earnings are in the news or there's a flood of strongly worded headlines.
    • These news rules haven't been proven to help yet. Every forecast is saved and graded once its 5 trading days are over.
    • This is a research tool, not financial advice.
